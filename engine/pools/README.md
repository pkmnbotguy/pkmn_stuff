# Team pools

## Teams_cov

418 teams for Champions VGC Reg M-C, generated by `tools/coverage_pool.js` from Showdown's dex. Together they field:

- every legal species (293 species and forms);
- every legal species-ability pair (722);
- every learnable move (510 of 514);
- every legal item (166).

Every team passes Showdown's team validator for `gen9championsvgc2026regmc`.

The pool is built for coverage, not for strength: sets are legal and plausible, not tournament-tuned. It exercises
the engine on content that real teams rarely bring, and it is the default pool for the fuzzers and benchmarks.

Regenerate it:

```bash
node tools/coverage_pool.js $SHOWDOWN_DIR gen9championsvgc2026regmc pools/Teams_cov 1 --copies 6
```

Format: Showdown's team export text. `EVs:` values are Champions stat points (66 total, at most 32 per stat).

## Your own teams

Any folder of Showdown export files works. Point `ED_TEAMS` at it for the tools, or read the files yourself in
Python. The speed figures in the main README were measured with tournament teams. The coverage pool runs a little
slower because its games use more unusual effects.
